# E04 exploratory local ASR

Original source crop was used for style reference. Local cached no-prompt Hindi ASR recovers approximate verse and chorus text through 76.32 s, then enters a repeated-token loop. The tail is unusable for lyric/ending judgments. ASR does not measure style transfer, voice identity, melody preservation or novelty. See `asr_hi_fallback.json` and `research/AUDIO_EVALUATION.md`.
