## Suggested listening order

Yamuna, Daylight, original Hindi song, then the hybrid. Compare the last two
for the musicality-versus-intelligibility problem. Listen to the three remixes
to understand why raw processing success was insufficient for the original brief.

The original user remix goal was: recognisable but substantially transformed,
with coherent transitions rather than sequential playback or random fragments.
The rejected remix examples contain transformed supplied excerpts from existing
works; they are private context examples, not original compositions we claim
to own or assets to publish in the new app's demo library.

Original generated music examples and their recipes are useful local references.
A future app's public showcase still requires a separate product/content decision.

Do not claim to hear these if your environment cannot play/analyze audio.
Earshot can support broad acoustic descriptions; it cannot replace the recorded
listener feedback, certify lyrics or establish performance virtuosity.

`AUDIO_CATALOGUE.json` provides exact source paths, hashes, durations, formats,
feedback and notes. All nine playable examples are copied into this folder;
they are not links to missing local files. Original WAV masters remain at
their source locations on this Mac and are not duplicated in the portable pack.
