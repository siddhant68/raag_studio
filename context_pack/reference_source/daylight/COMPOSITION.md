## Musical brief

Melodious, warm and gently uplifting, with a memorable refrain and a steady,
unhurried rhythm. No spoken narration or lyrical verses: the human-voice part
is a wordless ooh/aah chorus.

Requested instrumentation: lyrical piano, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, flute,
legato strings/cello, rounded bass, restrained kick/brushed snare/shaker and
blended choir. The production aims for clear melodic foreground and a soft
rhythmic foundation rather than a dense wall of sound.

## Planned development

The generation brief specifies an intimate piano opening, flowing instrumental
verse, broader choir chorus, a piano/cello reduction and an expanded final
chorus, followed by a resolved piano ending. Return of the same hook is meant
to unify the arrangement, with contrasting register and instrumentation.

Metadata requests 88 BPM, D major, 4/4 and 132 seconds. These are generation
conditions, not independently verified score properties or guaranteed section
boundaries. The model renders the notes and performance; no hand-notated
score, separate instrument stems or exact note-to-voice timeline is claimed.

## Production

ACE-Step 1.5 turbo, existing local MLX/MPS installation, optional music LM off.
Two complete performances with seeds 24102601 and 24102602 are preserved in
`takes/`, with full requests, results and native audio. Prompt and wordless
vocal structure are in `caption.txt` and `wordless_vocals.txt`.

This uses the established native Python inference route documented in the
earlier installation guide, adapted from the official REST-oriented skill.
No source song recording, named singer imitation, new model installation or
paid generation service is used. Sonic Pi is not used to render this piece.

Planning drew on the downloaded `mc-melody` and `mc-arrangement-arch` guides:
recurring motives, breathing phrases, contrast through removing instruments,
and a later high point. Earshot descriptions support broad acoustic checks;
their scores cannot establish musical beauty or listener preference.

Final selection, mastering settings, checks and listening limits are recorded
alongside the finished exports. Earlier accepted and rejected songs remain
untouched.
