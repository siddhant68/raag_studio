# थोड़ी देर और — original Hindi romantic song

Intent before lyric drafting:
- Type: situational, lyrics first.
- One thing: I want this ordinary evening with you to last a little longer.
- Addressee: the lover beside the window, about to leave after tea.
- Moment: the brief pause when she turns back toward the speaker at sunset.
- Anchor object: a half-full white ceramic teacup on a small wooden table.
- Anchor request: थोड़ी देर और, यहीं ठहर.
- Perspective: first person, directly addressing her in the present.
- Withholding: whether she stays is left unanswered.

Direction: Hindi romantic acoustic pop ballad; intimate first verse, a broad
melodic chorus, restrained drums, guitar and piano, a quieter bridge and final
chorus. Warm expressive male lead with clear Hindi diction, without targeting
any named singer's voice. 76 BPM, 4/4, working key D major; about 2:32.

Reference study: Kabir Singh's romantic soundtrack, especially the direct
address, longing and repeated emotional refrain of Tujhe Kitna Chahne Lage.
The new lyrics, melody and generated performance are original material, not
an adaptation of its words or tune. No reference audio is supplied to the model.
